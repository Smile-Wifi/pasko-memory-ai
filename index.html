Helpful answers

A few things to know

What kind of photo should I upload?

Choose one clear photo where the face is easy to see. JPG, PNG, and WEBP images up to 10 MB are accepted.

How many Christmas photos are included?

Both packages include five Christmas portraits in the styles shown above.

Which package includes music?

Magic with Music includes a slideshow with music. Basic Magic includes five photos without background music.

How do I pay?

Choose GCash, Maya, or bank transfer when you place your order. The studio reviews payment before creating your photos.
